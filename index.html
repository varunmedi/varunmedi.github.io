ABOUT

Designing intuitive products, end to end

I'm a UX / Product Designer with 6+ years of experience designing complex digital products, enterprise SaaS, and collaboration platforms across fintech, banking, insurance, and automotive. I specialize in interaction design, design systems, and end-to-end product design in Agile environments.

I work fluently in Figma, prototyping, and design systems — creating high-fidelity mockups, interactive prototypes, and reusable component libraries that keep products consistent and streamline design-to-development handoff. I care deeply about accessibility (WCAG) and inclusive design, shipping responsive experiences across web, desktop, and mobile for tens of thousands of users.

I partner closely with product, engineering, and research teams — translating complex requirements into intuitive, scalable design solutions and validating them through usability testing and UX research. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Springfield and a Bachelor's in Computer Science from Andhra University.
